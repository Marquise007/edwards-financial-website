Sales Navigator Recipe Book

San Diego, Orange County and Ventura County. Four segments each: K-12 teachers, first responders, county and city staff, colleges. Every search is built on Current company = employer page, because the employer decides the pension system. Type anything below to filter: a district, a department, a title, a pension system.

Work order inside every search

  1. Toggle Posted on LinkedIn first. That is the active minority; warm them with comments before connecting.
  2. Connection = 2nd degree next. Mutual-connection requests are the strongest opener (playbook template C).
  3. Save the remainder into a Lead list per cluster so the note can say "I work with [district or department] people."
  4. Changed jobs toggle as its own signal list: movers and leavers get the portability message (templates D and E).
  5. Save every search with alerts on so new matches flow weekly.

Sales Navigator shows at most 2,500 results per search (100 pages); Connections-of searches about 1,000. Tenure buckets are 1-2, 3-5, 6-10 and 10+ years. Any search that shows 2.5K+ needs splitting.

Skip Seniority, Function and Industry filters entirely. Public sector profiles map to them inconsistently and every extra ANDed filter deletes real people. Ignore the suggested chips; in Richard's session they were Dallas, Entry Level, Sales and Korn Ferry.
