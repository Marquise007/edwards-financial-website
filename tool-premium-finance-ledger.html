Method and sources

What this module computes, what it takes from a source document, and what it deliberately refuses to do.

The loan engine, verified

VerifiedEach year the bank charges interest on the prior balance plus that year's new premium. The client's fixed cash payment is subtracted and any shortfall capitalizes. Reproduced against nineteen printed years to a maximum error of ninety two cents, which is rounding in the source document.

VerifiedRequired outside collateral is the loan balance less the advance rate applied to surrender value, floored at zero.

VerifiedAt the exit year the bank balance is repaid with a policy loan equal to the prior year end balance grown one year at the policy loan charge rate. That loan then compounds and is not repaid.

VerifiedUnder benefit option B the gross death benefit is the specified amount plus the account value, constant across every pre exit year in the source document.

The policy side, not modeled

Account value and surrender value are inputs, not outputs. In the source case study the account value compounds at rates between 5.61 and 6.07 percent net of charges against a stated illustrated rate of 5.5 percent. The cause of that difference is not disclosed in the document, so reproducing the policy engine would require assumptions this module will not invent. Paste real illustration values.

Sketch mode exists only to shape a scenario before an illustration is in hand. It labels itself as an approximation everywhere it appears, and no figure it produces belongs in a client document.

Carrier gates this module does not enforce

The twenty percent client contribution test is computed and displayed, and it reflects one carrier's published guideline. Every other gate belongs to the carrier: issue age bands, net worth floors, maximum non modified endowment funding, approved vendor and lender lists, disclosure forms, producer training, and the requirement that a case be financially justified independent of the financing. None of them are checked here.

UnverifiedGift exposure depends on the current annual exclusion and lifetime exemption amounts. This module supplies neither. Confirm both against primary source before any figure leaves this screen.
