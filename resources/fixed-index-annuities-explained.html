Fixed Index Annuities, Explained Clearly

Fixed index annuities are best understood as a complete structure, not a collection of isolated benefits. A sound evaluation should consider growth potential, principal protection, income options, access, costs, and tradeoffs together, so you can decide whether the structure fits your broader retirement plan.

What to listen for

Use the video to understand the basic structure and the role an FIA may play, not to make a decision by itself.

  1. 1 Protection How principal and previously credited interest are protected from market downturns.
  2. 2 Growth potential How fixed and index-based crediting options may add interest without direct market ownership.
  3. 3 Income & tradeoffs How income choices, withdrawals, surrender periods, taxes, and rider costs may affect results.
